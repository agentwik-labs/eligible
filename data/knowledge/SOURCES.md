## Notes

- **p502**: Written for the itemized deduction. Its rule that non-prescribed drugs aren't includible does not apply to HSA/FSA reimbursement after 2019 (see p969, cares).
- **p502pdf**: Same content as the HTML edition. Kept for reference, not indexed.
- **cares**: congress.gov returned 403 to automated download; govinfo.gov serves the same official public law. §3702 makes OTC medicine eligible by *striking* the sentence that limited medicines to prescribed drugs and insulin; the only new text is about menstrual care products. Retrieval must pair it with p969 to explain OTC verdicts.
- **n2013-71**: Not needed for verdicts. Indexed on purpose as an off-topic distractor.
